Community fundraising

The One Million Steps for OCD Walk

Every year, the International OCD Foundation's OCD Walk brings people touched by OCD together to walk side by side, tell their stories, and raise both funds and understanding for the OCD and related-disorders community. The walk began in memory of a young man lost to OCD and body dysmorphic disorder, and it's grown into one of the largest grassroots events in the field — proceeds go directly toward education, provider training, and outreach.

NECOA is proud to be a 2026 OCD Walk sponsor, and our clinicians and clients turn out every year to walk in support of the same mission that drives our clinical work.

04Community Impact

The reach of a growing network.

What eight clinics and decades of specialized care add up to, across the communities we serve.

8 clinics

From Melrose to Los Angeles to County Meath, Ireland — each staffed by clinicians trained in evidence-based OCD and anxiety care.

5 U.S. states + Ireland

Massachusetts, Maine, Rhode Island, New York, and California, plus our clinic in the Irish Midlands.

Decades of expertise

Our clinicians trained at McLean Hospital's OCD Institute, Harvard Medical School, and Boston University's Center for Anxiety and Related Disorders.

Access for families

Sliding-scale options, scholarship spots for our Complex Consultation Service, and free monthly Information Hours.

Growing a field, not just a caseload.

Every clinician we train, every family we walk alongside, and every free resource we publish is part of the same mission: making specialized OCD and anxiety care easier to find, everywhere.
